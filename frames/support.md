## Common questions

### How do I add the Frames widget to my Home Screen?

After customizing your widget in Frames, go to your iPhone Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, tap **Edit** or **+**, choose **Add Widget**, search for Frames, and add the widget size you want.

### My widget did not update immediately

iOS controls widget refresh timing. Open Frames, confirm your preview looks correct, then wait a moment for iOS to refresh the Home Screen widget. Removing and re-adding the widget can also force a refresh.

### How many content items can I show?

Frames supports up to three content slots. Some widget layouts may visually emphasize two slots more strongly, but your selected order and selected items are saved in the App.

### How do I change the order of widget items?

Open Frames, go to Customize, and reorder the selected content slots. The widget uses the order saved in the App.

### Calendar, birthday, or Health data looks wrong

Check that the matching permission is granted in iOS Settings. Then open Frames and refresh or edit your selected slots. Calendar, Contacts, and Health data are read on your device only.

### How do I test or manage purchases?

Frames Pro purchases are handled by Apple. To manage subscriptions, open iOS Settings, tap your Apple ID, then **Subscriptions**. To request a refund, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### I bought Frames Pro but it is not unlocked

Open Frames, go to Settings, and tap **Restore Purchases** while signed into the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

### How do I delete custom AI themes?

Open the Themes tab and delete custom themes you created. If the active theme is deleted, Frames falls back to a default theme.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete custom themes/backgrounds inside Frames, reset settings where available, or uninstall the App. App data is stored locally on your device and in the widget App Group container.

### I found a bug. What should I include?

Email [danmoriyt@gmail.com](mailto:danmoriyt@gmail.com) with:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead
- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- The widget size and theme
- A screenshot or screen recording if helpful
