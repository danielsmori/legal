# Privacy Policy — Frames

**Last updated:** June 2, 2026
**Effective date:** Upon installation

This Privacy Policy explains how Frames ("we", "the App"), published by Daniel Mori, handles information when you use the Frames iOS app.

Frames is designed to keep your widget customization data on your device. We do not sell personal information, track you for advertising, or operate a backend service for this App.

## Summary

| Data | Why it is used | Where it goes | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Widget settings and selected theme | To render your app preview and widgets | Stored on your device and in the shared App Group container | Until you change/reset settings or uninstall the App |
| Custom themes and backgrounds | To show your saved custom widget styles | Stored on your device and in the shared App Group container | Until you delete them, reset settings, or uninstall the App |
| Photos you select | To create a custom background or AI-inspired theme | Processed on device | Not uploaded by us |
| Calendar events | To show the next event slot you choose | Read on device only | Cached locally for widget display |
| Contacts birthdays | To show the birthdays slot you choose | Read on device only | Cached locally for widget display |
| Health data | To show wellness slots you choose | Read on device only | Cached locally for widget display |
| Purchase status | To unlock Frames Pro features | Apple App Store / StoreKit | Retained by Apple according to Apple's policies |

We do **not** collect, store, or sell:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
- Advertising identifiers
- Precise location
- Calendar, contact, photo, or Health data on any server controlled by us
- AI prompts, generated themes, or widget content on any server controlled by us

## 1. Information the App Uses

### 1.1 Widget settings

Frames stores your selected theme, widget layout, chosen content slots, custom message, countdown settings, and related preferences locally. This data is also written to the App Group container `group.com.danielmori.frames` so the widget extension can render your widget.

### 1.2 Custom themes and backgrounds

If you save a custom theme or background, Frames stores it locally and in the App Group container so it can appear in the app and widget. You can delete custom themes and remove backgrounds from inside the App.

### 1.3 Photos

Frames may ask you to choose a photo when you create a custom background or build a theme from a photo. The photo is processed on your device. We do not receive, upload, or store your photos on our servers.

### 1.4 Calendar

If you enable a calendar-based widget slot, Frames asks for calendar access and reads upcoming events on your device. Calendar data is used only to display the widget slot you choose.

### 1.5 Contacts

If you enable a birthdays widget slot, Frames asks for contacts access and reads birthday fields on your device. Contact data is used only to display upcoming birthdays in your widget.

### 1.6 Health

If you enable wellness widget slots, Frames asks for permission to read the relevant Health data types. Health data is used only to display the widget slots you choose. Frames does not write Health data.

### 1.7 AI features

Frames includes AI-assisted theme and quote features. When Apple's on-device Foundation Models are available, generation happens on your device. If on-device generation is unavailable, Frames uses curated local fallbacks. We do not receive or store your AI prompts or generated themes on our servers.

### 1.8 Purchases

Frames Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We receive only the purchase entitlement state needed to unlock features in the App. We never see your credit card number, Apple ID password, or billing details.

## 2. How We Use Information

Frames uses information only to:

- Render and update your widget
- Save your customization choices
- Show optional content slots you choose
- Unlock Frames Pro after purchase
- Diagnose issues you report to us

We do not use your information for advertising, cross-app tracking, or sale to data brokers.

## 3. Sharing of Information

Frames does not share your app content, widget settings, photos, calendar data, contact data, or Health data with us or third-party advertising services.

Purchases are handled by Apple App Store / StoreKit. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policies.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Frames is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at [danmoriyt@gmail.com](mailto:danmoriyt@gmail.com).

## 5. Your Choices

You can:

- Delete custom themes and backgrounds in the App
- Reset app settings in the App where available
- Revoke Calendar, Contacts, Photos, or Health permissions in iOS Settings
- Manage purchases and subscriptions through your Apple ID settings
- Uninstall the App to remove local app data from your device

## 6. Data Retention

Local widget settings, custom themes, backgrounds, and cached slot data remain on your device until you change them, reset them, delete them, or uninstall the App.

Purchase records are retained by Apple according to Apple's policies.

## 7. Security

Frames uses Apple's platform storage and permission systems. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but the App is intentionally designed to avoid collecting sensitive data on servers controlled by us.

## 8. International Users

Frames is available internationally. Because the App stores its functional data locally and does not operate a user-data backend, international transfer of widget content to us is not part of normal App operation.
